      Quick guide

      How to Play

      On a phone or tablet. You are placed at your ball automatically. Drag left or right on the course to aim, tap a club in the tray, then hold the big SWING button for power, let go, and tap it again in the gold zone. ☰ pauses or restarts the hole.
      1. Get to your ball. Press F to jump straight to it, or walk with WASD (Shift to jog, arrows or right-drag to look around). Press E when you are close.
      2. Pick and aim. Keys 1 Driver, 2 3-Wood, 3 7-Iron, 4 Wedge, 5 Putter. The yellow Coach pick badge shows the best club for each shot. You start aimed at the flag. Hold A/D, the arrow keys, or move your mouse to adjust (hold Shift for fine aim).
      3. Make the swing. Hold the mouse or Space for power, release, then click the gold accuracy zone.
      4. Finish the hole. Play every next shot from where the ball stops. A low score wins. Water and out of bounds add one stroke.
      Putting tip. Every green has its own hills. When you set up with the Putter, little dots flow downhill: the faster they move, the more the ball will curve. The line only shows the start of your putt, so aim up the hill and let it roll down to the cup.
